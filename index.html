The Problem Statement

Why SageCure was Conceived

Healthcare diagnostics in India and globally suffer from chronic fragmentation, medical jargon barriers, dangerous AI hallucinations, and unstructured paper records that jeopardize patient outcomes.

🧩
1. Fragmented Lab Report Interpretation & Jargon Anxiety

Over 80% of patients receiving lab reports (e.g. lipid panels, CBC, HbA1c) struggle to understand abbreviations and complex ranges. This leads to intense health anxiety, unnecessary self-medication, or dangerous complacency. SageCure translates dense pathology reports into clear, bilingual, empathetic guidance while preserving clinical precision.

⚠️
2. Lethal AI Hallucination & Fabrication in Healthcare

Standard large language models (LLMs) tend to fabricate plausible numbers when context is missing. If a patient says "I have fever", naive LLMs often hallucinate a temperature of 101°F or normal blood pressure of 120/80 mmHg. SageCure introduces a deterministic clinical rule engine: missing vitals are strictly prohibited from being fabricated and are flagged as Unknown / Unmeasured.

🔒
3. Insecure Patient Data Management & Cross-Session Data Bleed

Many health web apps store records in unsegmented local storages or shared demo sessions, causing sensitive lab histories to leak between users on shared clinic or family devices. SageCure guarantees zero demo pre-fills, completely clean blank login forms for new visitors, and cryptographically isolated per-user session storage.

✍️
4. Illegible Handwritten Prescriptions & Dosage Errors

Adverse drug reactions and dispensing errors frequently stem from illegible physician handwriting. SageCure's Prescription Medication Giver standardizes digital prescribing with structured dosage timings (OD, BD, TID), meal-relative instructions, and verifiable digital signatures compliant with NMC Telemedicine Guidelines 2026.

🇮🇳
5. Lack of Interoperability with India's ABDM Ecosystem

India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) requires clinical records to be exchanged as structured HL7 FHIR v4 JSON bundles. SageCure maps all extracted biomarkers directly to standardized LOINC codes and NRCeS diagnostic profiles, enabling longitudinal health record exchange.

Technical Architecture

End-to-End System Workflow

SageCure orchestrates a high-performance modern web client, a robust Node.js API gateway, an automated Make.com LLM reasoning pipeline, Supabase cloud persistence, and strict FHIR v4 data models.

1
Document Ingestion & Hybrid OCR:

The user uploads a PDF or lab photo, or clicks a 1-click sample preset. The frontend performs client-side OCR via Tesseract.js and forwards multipart payloads to the Node.js API gateway (/api/upload) for server-side text extraction using pdf-parse.

2
Deterministic Emergency & Vitals Guardrails:

Before AI execution, deterministic regex/keyword screeners inspect patient queries. If emergency keywords are found, the Red Emergency Banner flashes instantly without API latency. If symptoms lack measured vitals, temperature/BP are locked to Unknown.

3
Make.com Webhook & LLM Reasoning Pipeline:

Payloads are dispatched to Make.com (hook.eu1.make.com/...) which triggers LLM reasoning modules. The AI extracts structured biomarker JSON, assigns clinical severity ratings (Optimal, Borderline, Critical), and generates patient explanations in English or Hindi.

4
Supabase Persistence & Isolated Local Storage:

Analyzed diagnostic records are saved into Supabase PostgreSQL and synced to isolated user storage (health_copilot_session_${userId}). Each user sees only their own health history with zero cross-contamination.

5
ABDM HL7 FHIR v4 Mapping Engine:

Clinical data is transformed in real-time into an NRCeS-compliant HL7 FHIR v4 Bundle with valid LOINC codes, practitioner references, and patient ABHA IDs ready for Ayushman Bharat Digital Health exchange.
